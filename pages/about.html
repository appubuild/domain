About DomainCraft

Better starting points for naming.

DomainCraft is a simple, browser-based tool and resource for people shaping a new project, product, or online identity.

What this site does

DomainCraft helps visitors explore combinations of words around a keyword. The generator offers controls for word length, placement, capitalization, and domain extensions, and lets visitors filter, copy, or export results. The blog provides practical guidance for evaluating a name after the brainstorming stage.

The tool is intentionally a starting point. It runs in the visitor's browser and does not check domain registrations, pricing, trademarks, social usernames, search results, or business-name records. A displayed idea should always be verified independently before it is used or registered.

Our approach to useful content

Our guides aim to explain concrete decisions and checks rather than promise a perfect name. We try to distinguish general information from legal advice, describe tool limitations, and give readers next steps they can verify.
