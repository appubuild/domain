1. Who is responsible

For privacy questions, the data controller is [insert the publisher's full legal or trading name and business address, if applicable] (“DomainCraft,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). Contact us through the contact page. The contact form needs a working delivery endpoint before it can send messages.

2. Information this starter site handles

  • Generator inputs: keyword, word-length, extension, and filter choices are processed in the visitor's browser. This starter code does not send those tool inputs to a DomainCraft server.
  • Theme preference: the light/dark selection may be stored in local browser storage under the key domaincraft-theme. It is used only to remember the display preference.
  • Contact messages: the form is a front-end template. Until a Formspree or other delivery endpoint is configured, it does not transmit or store a message. If a provider is enabled, the information entered (such as name, email address, topic, and message) will be sent to that provider and handled under its terms and privacy notices.
  • Hosting logs: the web host may process technical request data, such as IP address, request time, browser details, and requested files, to deliver and secure the site. Confirm the hosting provider and its log-retention practices before launch.

3. Why information may be used

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, the publisher should identify one for each purpose. Depending on the actual deployment, purposes may include responding to a message at your request, protecting the site from abuse, keeping the site functional, measuring performance, or displaying advertising. Do not enable a purpose or vendor that is not accurately disclosed here. The generator itself can be used without an account.

4. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

No Google Analytics or Google AdSense code is active in this starter project. The theme preference uses local storage rather than an advertising cookie. The hosting provider may use technically necessary mechanisms; check its documentation and update this notice if relevant.

If the publisher later enables Google Analytics, AdSense, or another third-party service, those providers may collect or receive device, browser, usage, identifier, or approximate location information and may use cookies or similar technologies. Google explains its advertising technologies at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Visitors can review Google advertising controls at My Ad Center. Add the exact services, purposes, retention settings, and consent choices used by this site before enabling them.

Where required by Google policy or local law, including for visitors in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, configure a Google-certified consent management platform and obtain the required consent before using relevant advertising or analytics technologies. A privacy-policy page alone does not collect consent.
